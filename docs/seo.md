## English

**Page title:** BetterSet — Gym workout log for Android

**Meta description:** BetterSet tracks gym sets, reps and weight, with workout history, body-weight charts, progress photos and local reminders for Android.

**Short description:** Log sets, reps and weight. Follow your training history, body weight and progress photos.

**Product description:** BetterSet is an Android gym workout log for recording sets, reps and working weight. Choose a built-in program, record each set in kilograms or pounds, and keep completed workouts in your history and calendar. Body-weight charts, photo comparisons, training statistics and awards help you review your activity over time. English and Russian interfaces, themes and local reminders let you adapt the app to your routine. BetterSet stores your training records on your phone and includes banner advertising.

[Download on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bettereachset.gymtracker).
